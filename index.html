The rewards platform for creators.

Winwrx, LLC builds and operates a rewards platform for creators, streamers, and podcasters. Every partner gets their own branded website and mobile app, with streams, comments, games, streaks, points, merchandise, giveaways, memberships, and a store with entries built in, all working together.

Fans follow their creator, play, shop, and enter giveaways in one place, and the creator earns every day instead of only when a new post goes out.

What we provide

  • Website and appA branded storefront on the web and in the app stores, carrying the creator's own name and look.
  • StoreMerchandise and digital items, with checkout, order handling, and shipping.
  • Games and pointsGames fans play for points, which they put toward giveaway entries.
  • GiveawaysSweepstakes with entries, drawings, winner notices, and the paperwork that follows.
  • MembershipsPaid monthly plans with recurring billing and member-only perks.
  • StreamsA creator's posts gathered from the places they already publish, kept in sync.

Text messages

Winwrx sends one-time sign-in codes by text message to people who sign in to a winwrx website or app with their mobile number. A code is sent only when the person asks for one, at the moment they ask. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the full program details.
